Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. Who We Are

zelandaliyoruk.com and its social media accounts are run as an individual project by Hasan, who lives in Tauranga, New Zealand. It is not a company. Hasan is the data controller and privacy officer. Contact: zelandaliyoruk@gmail.com. A postal address is provided on request where needed for a legal request.

This notice is provided under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and, for visitors in Turkey, as the information notice under Turkish Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

2. What We Collect

  • If you fill in the guide form: your full name and email address.
  • If you use the question form: your full name, email address, your question and its topic.
  • The submission date and a record of your consent.
  • Your IP address is not stored. To prevent spam, a short irreversible hash of it is kept temporarily and removed at the next form submission.
  • Your language preference, cookie choice and form status stay in your browser (localStorage) and are not sent to us.

Filling in the forms is optional. Without them you can still read the guide pages; you just can't send us a question.

3. Why We Use It

  • To answer your question. Selected questions may be answered in general terms in a video or on the site; your name and email are not published.
  • To protect the site from abuse and spam.
  • If you allow it, to understand which pages people find useful.

We do not send bulk updates or marketing emails. If that changes, we will ask for your permission separately. This site does not provide immigration, legal, tax or financial advice, and questions do not receive a personal visa assessment.

4. Legal Basis

Form data is processed on the basis of the explicit consent you give by ticking the box (KVKK Art. 5) and is collected directly from you (Privacy Act 2020). You can withdraw consent at any time; this doesn't affect processing before withdrawal.

5. Where Data Is Stored and Who Receives It

We don't sell your data or share it for marketing. The site uses these service providers:

  • GitHub (USA): form records, in a restricted private repository.
  • Vercel (USA): site hosting and form processing.
  • Google (USA): Google Analytics, only if you allow it.

Your data is therefore processed on servers outside New Zealand and Turkey, where the level of protection may differ. This transfer is based on the explicit consent you give in the form (KVKK Art. 9). Data may be disclosed to authorities where the law requires it.

6. How Long We Keep It

Form records are kept for up to 24 months after your last submission and then deleted. They are deleted sooner if you ask.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps such as restricted access and encrypted connections, but no system on the internet is completely secure. If a privacy breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify affected people and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

8. Cookies and Analytics

Vercel Web Analytics, according to its provider, collects anonymous visit statistics without cookies.

Google Analytics cookies are used only if you choose "Accept" in the cookie window. You can change your choice using the "Cookie preferences" link at the bottom of each page.

9. Your Rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can access information about you and ask for it to be corrected. If a complaint isn't resolved, you can contact the New Zealand Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz).

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you also have the right to:

  • Learn whether your data is processed and request information about it,
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly,
  • Know the third parties it is transferred to in Turkey or abroad,
  • Request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data, deletion where the conditions are met, and notification of these to recipients,
  • Object to an adverse result arising from automated analysis,
  • Claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

Send requests to zelandaliyoruk@gmail.com; we respond within 20 working days. If you are not satisfied, you can apply to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board (kvkk.gov.tr).

10. Age Limit

The forms are not intended for people under 16. If we become aware of such a record, we delete it.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version and its date are always on this page.
